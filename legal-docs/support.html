Common Questions

How do I subscribe to Pro?

Open the app, go to Profile → Upgrade to Pro, and choose your subscription plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription anytime through your iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → DadaKnows → Cancel Subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Please visit Apple's Report a Problem page or contact Apple Support directly.

Is my child's data safe?

Yes! We take privacy seriously and comply with COPPA standards. Please read our Privacy Policy for details.
